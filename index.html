Who We Are

About Alternate Mining & Industrial

Alternate Supplies, trading as Alternate Mining & Industrial, is a proudly Zimbabwean company established in 2015 with a clear mission: to deliver the essential spare parts that keep industries moving. From the relentless demands of mining operations deep underground to the seasonal intensity of agriculture across the fields, we provide solutions that ensure machines remain in motion, downtime is minimized, and productivity is sustained.

Our clients know that when they partner with us, they are not just purchasing parts — they are securing continuity, safeguarding performance, and investing in long-term value. We focus on the equipment our clients run today: LHDs, front-end loaders, drill rigs and dump trucks from Sandvik, Atlas Copco, GHH, RDT, CAT, XCMG, Bell and Hitachi. For each, we cover the systems that matter most — engines, transmissions, hydraulics, brakes and steering, axles, electricals — plus the filters, wear parts and lubricants used in routine maintenance.

Technical Capabilities

The systems we know inside out

This is what we supply for each major system on your machine, and the equipment it fits. You can see what we cover before you call us.

Engines & Power Systems

Diesel engines power everything we support, and underground they work in heat, dust and long shifts. We supply complete engine assemblies and overhaul components, from a fuel filter to a full rebuild kit.

What we supply
  • Complete & long-block engines
  • Cylinder heads, pistons, liners & rings
  • Injectors & fuel injection pumps
  • Turbochargers
  • Water pumps, radiators & coolers
  • Starters & alternators
  • Gasket sets & overhaul kits
  • Engine mounts & belts

Fits: Deutz engines common in underground LHDs and drill rigs, and the engines fitted to CAT, Hitachi, XCMG and Bell equipment.

Transmission & Drive Systems

The transmission sends engine power to the wheels. When it fails, the machine stops. We supply complete units and the internal parts needed to repair one.

What we supply
  • Powershift transmissions & gearboxes
  • Torque converters
  • Control valves & solenoids
  • Clutch packs, friction & steel plates
  • Transmission pumps & oil coolers
  • Propeller shafts & universal joints
  • Output yokes, flanges & mounts
  • Seal & rebuild kits

Fits: Powershift drivelines on LHDs, front-end loaders and dump trucks across all the brands we support.

Hydraulic Systems

Hydraulics drive the bucket, the boom, the steering and the brakes. Pressure loss or a burst hose stops work. We supply the pumps, valves, cylinders and hoses that keep pressure where it should be.

What we supply
  • Gear & piston pumps
  • Boom, bucket, hoist & steering cylinders
  • Directional & control valves
  • Hoses, fittings & adaptors
  • Cylinder seal kits
  • Accumulators
  • Hydraulic coolers
  • Return & pressure filters

Fits: Loader lift and tilt circuits, drill rig boom and feed systems, and truck hoist and steering circuits.

Brakes & Steering

Underground machines need brakes and steering that work every time. We supply parts for the spring-applied, hydraulic-release (SAHR) brakes used on mining equipment, and for articulated steering systems.

What we supply
  • Brake discs, plates & linings
  • Brake pads & calipers
  • Brake valves & accumulators
  • Park brake components
  • Steering cylinders
  • Steering units & valves
  • Articulation pins & bushes
  • Brake fluid & seal kits

Fits: Articulated LHDs, loaders and dump trucks, both underground and on the surface.

Axles, Driveline & Wheels

Axles carry the whole machine and its load. We supply complete axle assemblies and the internal and running-gear parts needed to rebuild them.

What we supply
  • Complete axle assemblies
  • Differentials, crown wheels & pinions
  • Planetary hubs & final drives
  • Bronze & oscillation bushes
  • Wheel rims & lock rings
  • Hub seals & bearings
  • Wheel studs & nuts
  • Tyres

Fits: Front and rear axles on LHDs, front-end loaders and dump trucks.

Electrical & Control Systems

Modern machines rely on sensors and control modules as much as on steel. We supply the electrical components that start the machine, monitor it and keep it safe.

What we supply
  • Starters & alternators
  • Pressure, temperature & speed sensors
  • Solenoids & relays
  • Wiring harnesses
  • Contactors & switches
  • Gauges & instrument panels
  • Work lights & beacons
  • Electric drill rig components

Fits: Diesel machines and electric-powered drill rigs.
