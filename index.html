Company Fundamentals

Built to bridge global manufacturers and local butcheries.

Alternate Supplies is a Zimbabwean-based distribution company. Our aim is to become a leading supplier in Southern Africa — reliable, cost-effective, and durable, for operations from small startups to industrial scale.

Our Mission

  • Supply high-quality, durable, and affordable equipment.
  • Provide excellent customer service and technical support.
  • Build long-term partnerships with clients in the food industry.
  • Bridge the gap between global manufacturers and local businesses.

Our Vision

To be Zimbabwe's most trusted and innovative supplier of food processing equipment and safety solutions — the name butcheries, bakeries, and abattoirs call first.
